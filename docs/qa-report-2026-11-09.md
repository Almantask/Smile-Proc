# QA report — 2026-11-09

Manual QA of the debug build (`com.example.smileproc.debug`, after the fragment 1.9.1 fix) on `sp36` (API 36) and `sp29` (API 29), driven with `scripts/emu/*` and uiautomator. Screenshots: `build/emu-shots/qa-*.png`, `e17-*.png`, `e18-*.png`.

## Summary

| Severity | Count |
|---|---|
| High | 3 (H-1, H-3 fixed) |
| Medium | 11 |
| Low | 10 |

Most core paths work: in-app and lock-screen saves, the one-notification rule, restore, swipe-away, standing on/off, test check-in, location capture, place naming, the weekly card variants, low-run support, export/import, delete all, reboot, dark mode and Android 10. The serious problems are in **App lock**, the **test check-in**, and a **crash after Delete all**.

---

## High

### H-1 · App lock isn't asked on launch (cold start, after reboot, Android 10 and 16) — **fixed**
- **Fix:** `AppLockManager` starts locked and decides the launch lock only from the real setting (`onSettingLoaded`); `MainActivity` shows nothing until DataStore answers; Delete all calls `markUserPresent()` instead of re-arming the lock. Tests: `AppLockLaunchTest`, `AppLockManagerTest`. Verified on sp36 (cold start, reboot, 15 s → no lock, 62 s → lock, 3× Delete all + onboarding → no early lock) and sp29 (cold start).
- **Steps:** App lock on. `am force-stop`, then open the app. Or reboot, unlock the phone, open the app.
- **Expected:** `BiometricPrompt` on launch (android.md › App lock).
- **Actual:** Today opens with full history and no prompt. Only the 60 s background timeout locks.
- **Cause:** [MainActivity.kt:229](../app/src/main/java/com/example/smileproc/MainActivity.kt) calls `AppLockManager.onResumeCheck(appLockEnabled)` on the first `onResume`, while `appLockEnabled` is still `false`. The real value arrives later from DataStore in a `LaunchedEffect`. `onResumeCheck(false)` clears `isFirstLaunch`, so the launch lock never happens. M9's E14 only tested the timeout path.
- **Related:**
  - After Delete all and onboarding, the lock fired after about 18 s in the background instead of 60 s (`isFirstLaunch` was reset).
  - After unlocking, the app returns to Today instead of the screen the user was on (Lock-screen check-in).

### H-2 · An untapped test check-in swallows a later real check-in
- **Steps:** Lock-screen check-in → Send a test check-in. Don't tap it. Minutes later, tap Good on the lock-screen notification.
- **Expected:** The entry is saved, or the test expires.
- **Actual:** The state stays `Test` indefinitely, until the next reminder. The test notification looks exactly like a real check-in ("How are you right now?"), so the later tap saves nothing and shows "It works ✓". Probe: entries 0 → 0.
- **Note:** This follows the spec table (logic.md: `Test` has no timeout; buttons "save nothing"), so it is a **spec gap that needs your decision**: for example, fall back to the resting state after N minutes, or treat a tap after a timeout as a real check-in. It conflicts with non-negotiables 6 and 7 (single tap saves; never a silent failure).

### H-3 · Delete all sometimes crashes the app — **fixed**
- **Fix:** each `StorageBundle` has a `StorageLifetime` that is marked closed before its database closes; repository `observe*` flows are created lazily and end quietly on a retired database (Room throws both on flow creation and on re-query). The weekly-card build effect logs `WEEKLY_CARD_BUILD_FAILED` instead of crashing. Test: `StorageCloseTest`. Verified: 3× seed-demo + Delete all on sp36, no crash.
- **Steps:** Settings → Delete all data → Delete everything.
- **Actual (1 of 2 runs):** `FATAL EXCEPTION … IllegalStateException: Database is closed` from `androidx.room3 … throwIfClosed`, raised inside a Compose `LaunchedEffect` (in the `MainApp` effects that query `storage`). The wipe itself completed: 0 entries, no notification, no alarms. The app restarts into onboarding when opened again.
- **Likely cause:** `AppContainer.startFresh()` closes the old database while `MainApp` is still composed with the old `storage`, so an effect, such as the weekly-card build effect, queries the closed database.

## Medium

### M-1 · The standing check-in doesn't come back after notification permission is granted again
- **Steps:** Revoke POST_NOTIFICATIONS in the background, reopen the app, then grant it (Lock-screen check-in → Allow notifications). All three readiness rows show ✓.
- **Actual:** There's no notification, although `standing` is on and the machine state is `Faces`. It stays missing through app opens and an in-app check-in, until `fire.sh reminder`. With no reminders set, it never comes back.
- **Fix idea:** Re-render the resting state when the permission is granted, or on resume when the state and the posted notification disagree.

### M-2 · After a skip or deny in onboarding, location can't be turned on from the app
- Capture location shows **ON** while the permission is missing. Toggling it requests nothing and explains nothing. android.md says "Settings explains" (for example, that approximate-only is too coarse). The only way back is the system app settings.

### M-3 · A weekly card is built for a week with no check-ins
- Fresh data on Monday Nov 9. Week shows an all-zero card for Nov 1–8 and the tab dot, instead of "Your first weekly card arrives Sunday at 19:00."
- **Cause:** In [MainApp.kt](../app/src/main/java/com/example/smileproc/ui/MainApp.kt) the build effect checks `entries.size >= firstCardMinEntries` over **all** entries, including ones made after `periodEnd`. logic.md: the first card needs the minimum number of entries *at* the card time.

### M-4 · "Remove location from all entries" leaves place patterns in weekly cards
- After confirming ("Places and place names go away"), stored cards still show "Check-ins at one of your places run lower…" with a place tip.

### M-5 · Reminders can't be edited
- Rows aren't tappable. To change a time or its days you have to delete the reminder and add it again. The spec says the person can "change, add or delete" reminders.
- Duplicate times are accepted (two 08:00 rows, using one of the 6 slots), and the list isn't sorted (08:15 after 20:00).

### M-6 · Import keeps only the latest weekly card
- Before export, Week had 4 past cards. After Delete all and Import, only the current week's card exists. android.md says cards "are rebuilt after import"; past weeks aren't.

### M-7 · Test check-in hint shows too early and is lost
- "No tap came through. Check that your lock screen…" appears right after tapping Send, together with "Lock your phone now", before the user could tap anything.
- `testRequestedByMe` uses `remember`, so after leaving and returning (the case the spec describes) the hint is gone.

### M-8 · Accessibility: selected state isn't exposed
These chips and faces show selection only visually; uiautomator reports `selected=false` / `checked=false` even when selected, so TalkBack can't announce it:
- tag chips in the list picker (the picker the spec reserves for TalkBack)
- the mood faces in entry detail
- the day chips in Add a reminder and in Weekly card time

### M-9 · Accessibility: entry rows don't say the mood
- Rows in Today and History have no text or content description for the face. TalkBack hears only "9:17 PM".

### M-10 · Week day strip
- Moods are told apart only by green/yellow/red dots with no legend or faces. That's colour as the only signal, and the traffic-light palette grades moods ("every face is a valid answer").
- The colours don't match the faces elsewhere (lavender/pink).
- At 200% font the numbers inside the dots are clipped (E17 fail, `e17-week.png`).
- At any size the day letters sit at different heights depending on how many dots a column has.

### M-11 · Accessibility: unlabelled controls
- The time steppers "+" and "−" (Add a reminder, Weekly card time) have no descriptions; TalkBack can't tell hour from minute.
- The onboarding App lock switch has an empty description.

## Low

1. **Wheel:** the selection dot sits over the label ("Peace•ful", "Lone•ly"), and the selected outline is clipped at the right edge.
2. **Picker mode labels are confusing:** a "Feelings wheel" title with a "Feelings" link and a "Detailed" link. At 200% font the list is shown but the label still says "Detailed".
3. **Mixed formats:** 12-hour "9:17 PM" in entries vs 24-hour "08:00" / "19:00" in settings; "11/8/26" chips vs "Nov 1, 2026" headers. "Past weeks" also includes the current card.
4. **Lock-screen check-in screen:** tapping the "Keep a check-in on the lock screen" label does nothing (only the switch works), unlike Settings. The heading and the button both read "Send a test check-in".
5. **Copy:**
   - The "Forget this name" dialog's button says "Delete".
   - Weekly card time is labelled "Days" for a single day.
   - "Try one small change at an unnamed place" reads awkwardly.

   Copy-reviewer to confirm.
6. **Navigation:** the Settings scroll position resets after opening a sub-screen.
7. **About** shows no version number.
8. **Overview live tile:** with App lock on, recents opened from *inside* the app shows full content (the launcher's live tile), and Overview's Screenshot button can capture it. From the home screen the card is correctly blank. This meets the spec (`setRecentsScreenshotEnabled`); `FLAG_SECURE` would close the gap. Your decision.
9. **Overlapping cards:** changing the card time to Monday 09:00 immediately built a Nov 2–9 card next to the existing Nov 1–8 card.
10. **Face colours:** in the light theme, Low and Good have nearly the same lavender fill.
